# Foamie Rescue: Brand

First draft, 2026-10-25. Goes with [overview.md](overview.md) and [foamie-rescue-spec.md](foamie-rescue-spec.md).

## Inspiration

| Source | What we take from it |
|---|---|
| [Annenberg PetSpace](https://annenbergpetspace.org/) | The warm, happy side of adoption. A big, friendly call to action ("Adopt a pet!"). Every animal has a name, a photo, and a short profile. It's a community place, not just a listing site. |
| [Patagonia Action Works](https://www.patagonia.com/actionworks/home/explore/all?center=34,-118.43) | A map that shows what's happening **near you** (this link is centered on LA). It connects people to local action. The cause matters more than the store. |
| [Patagonia core values](https://www.patagonia.com/core-values/) | Short, direct values that start with a verb ("Protect our home planet."). Speaks as "we". Honest about impact. Has a playful side ("Do it our way."). Keeps gear in use instead of buying new. |

## In one line

**Rescue shelter warmth + Patagonia backbone.** Happy and welcoming on the surface, serious about the planet underneath.

## Our values (draft)

Short and verb-first, the Patagonia way:

1. **Keep foam in the water.** Every foamie that already exists should get surfed before a new one gets made.
2. **Free, always.** No fees, no deposits, no catch.
3. **Every board has a story.** Boards get a name and a profile, like a rescue pup.
4. **Be a good neighbor.** Show up on time, be honest about dings, and pass it on when you're done.
5. **Build, don't buy.** Broken boards become blanks for real, long-lasting boards.

## Voice

- Warm, plain, and a little playful. Talk like a friendly surfer at the beach, not a company.
- Say "we" and "you."
- Short sentences. No guilt-tripping. Celebrate every rescue instead of shaming buyers.
- Example lines:
  - "Adopt a foamie. Free, forever."
  - "Meet Big Blue. 8ft, a few dings, loves small days."
  - "Big Blue found a home!"
  - "Before you buy one, adopt one."
  - "Ready to surf as-is?" (yes / no)

## Look and feel (ideas)

- **Board profile cards** like pet adoption cards: big photo, name, town, "Ready to surf" or "Upcycle only" tag, and an "I'd like this board" button.
- **A map of boards near you**, like Action Works, starting with Orange County.
- **Real photos** of real boards and people at real beaches. No polished stock photos.
- **Colors:** sunny and beachy, with one strong accent for buttons. To be explored.
- **"Rescued" badge** for adopted boards, and the same mark on the board sticker later.
