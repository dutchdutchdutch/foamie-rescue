# Foamie Rescue

## Purpose

Cut the making of new soft-top foam boards ("foamies"), the most toxic kind of board, by giving the ones that already exist a second home. We are not against surfboards, just against new foamies.

## Goals

- Make picking up a free foamie easy. This is what we lead with.
- Make giving one away feel good.
- Keep boards going around, so each one replaces a new one being bought.

## The idea

Foamie Rescue is a free matchmaking service, like a pet rescue. People who have a foamie meet people who want one.

- **Our own brand.** Never on Facebook. We only use Facebook to spread the word.
- **Website, and maybe a texting line.** People at the beach have a phone, not a laptop.
- **Every board has a story.** Each one gets a name and a profile. When it is adopted, the donor gets a photo of the new owner.
- **A sticker on every board.** "Rescued foamie. Text BOARD to (number)." The board is its own ad.
- **Easy to give back.** When you're done, text "DONE" and the board gets listed again.
- **Always free.** No fees, no deposits.
- **No physical drop-off spots.** Givers and takers arrange pickup themselves.

## Where we start

Orange County first, then LA and maybe San Diego.

## Riskiest guess

That enough people in one area will list boards so the site never feels empty.

## Not yet

No building yet. This file is the plan so far, from the first brainstorm (2026-10-25).
