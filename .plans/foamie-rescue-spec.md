# Foamie Rescue: Version 1 Spec

From the interview on 2026-10-25. Builds on [overview.md](overview.md).

## In one line

A free website that matches people who have an unwanted foamie with people who want one, so fewer new soft-tops get made.

## Who it's for

- **Givers:** people with a foamie in the garage they no longer use.
- **Takers:** beginners, families, and anyone about to buy a cheap new soft-top.
- **Upcyclers:** hobby backyard shapers who use old foam as a blank to shape a real, long-lasting surfboard (not another foamie).

## What's in version 1

- Website only. Texting comes later (see Roadmap).
- Launch area: Orange County. Then LA and maybe San Diego.
- Always free. No fees, no deposits.
- Hosting on a free plan (Firebase or Vercel). This is a hobby project for now.

## Listing a board (giver)

1. Giver confirms their phone **or** email (at least one).
2. Giver adds:
   - **Photo** (required). No photo, no listing. We accept losing some people over this.
   - **Town and neighborhood or zip** (required). Never the exact address.
   - **"Ready to surf as-is?"** yes / no (required). Wording to be made friendlier.
   - Length, brand/model, notes (optional). Size is usually clear from the photo.
3. Each board gets a name and a little profile (e.g. "Big Blue").

**Board tags**
- Every board is tagged **upcycle potential** by default.
- A board marked "not ready to surf" is **upcycle only**. Visitors can also flag a board as broken.

## Asking for a board (taker)

1. Taker confirms their phone or email.
2. Taker taps **"I'd like this board"** and answers:
   - How soon can you pick it up?
   - When: **anytime**, or pick **3-hour time windows**, plus an optional note to be more specific.
3. The giver gets an email and says yes or no.
4. When both have said yes, each gets the other's phone or email. They agree on the exact spot and time directly.
5. A giver can say yes to more than one taker. Whoever shows up first gets the board. (Two people showing up at once is unlikely.)

## After the handoff

- About a day after a match, we email both people: **"Did it find a home?"**
- We ask the taker: **"Would you have bought a new one?"** This is how we count new foamies not bought.
- We ask the taker for a photo with the board, and send it to the giver. This is the feel-good moment.
- Confirmed handoffs close the listing.

## Listings that sit

- A listing expires after **30 days**.
- At **day 20**, the giver gets a reminder.
- The giver can **relist** it, or confirm it's **gone / no longer available**.
- Surf seasons don't match spring cleaning, so relisting should be one tap.

## Reports and safety

- Anyone can report a listing (flipper, fake, not a foamie).
- A report **pauses** the listing and puts it in a **review queue** for the owner (Dutch).
- If a listing is removed, the giver's phone or email is **blocked**.
- Against flippers: honor system plus the report button. No per-person limit in version 1.
- Listings never show an exact location.

## Roadmap (after version 1)

- **Texting line.** Givers list by texting a photo and a few words. Note: texting costs a small amount (about $1–2 a month per number plus about a cent per text), and US numbers need registration, which can take a few weeks.
- **Board stickers.** "Rescued foamie. Text BOARD to (number)." Once a board's done, the owner texts DONE to relist it.
- LA and San Diego.
